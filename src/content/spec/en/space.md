# Open Hours

Outside of scheduled events, Secret Shop opens several evenings each week as a relaxed drop-in space for members of the creative community.

Open Hours are intentionally informal.

Bring a project, meet other creators, ask questions, collaborate, or simply spend time working in a creative environment.

No registration is required unless otherwise noted.

# During Open Hours

Open Hours are intentionally flexible.

Some evenings are quiet work sessions.

Others become opportunities to share projects, meet collaborators, play games, exchange ideas, or simply spend time with other creative people.

You're welcome to stay for as little or as long as you'd like during opening hours.

---

# Private Bookings

Outside of regular Open Hours and scheduled events, Secret Shop is available for private bookings.

The space is intended for creative, technical, educational, and maker-focused groups whose activities align with the spirit of the Secret Shop community.

Examples include:

* Team meetings
* Study groups
* Workshops
* Game development teams
* Art and animation sessions
* Music production
* Board game design
* Creative meetups

Daytime bookings and full-day reservations are available, subject to availability.

If you're unsure whether your activity is a good fit, we'd be happy to hear from you.
