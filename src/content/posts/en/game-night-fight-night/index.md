**SS: GameNight: Fight Night** is a casual evening for fighting games, versus games, team battles, rotations, and friendly competition at Secret Shop.

Come to play, watch, learn, meet other players, or bring a setup. The focus is relaxed PvP matches, fighting games, and friendly competition, with optional mini-brackets or team battles if enough people want to play.

You are welcome to bring your own USB or wireless controller, laptop, handheld, or PvP game setup.

[comment]: <FeaturedDescription>


## Event Flow

| Time | Activity |
|---|---|
| `18:00` | Doors open / casual free play |
| `19:00` | Fight Night rotations or featured games begin |
| `21:00` | Optional mini-bracket, team battle, or finals |
| `22:00 - 23:00` | Casual matches and cooldown |

This is not intended to be a serious tournament-first event. The format can stay casual or become more structured depending on who shows up and what people want to play.

---

## What To Bring

You do not need to bring anything to attend.

Optional things to bring:

- USB controller or wireless controller
- Laptop or handheld console
- Fighting game setup you want people to try
- A game you want to play with others
- Headphones, charger, adapter, or dock if your setup needs one

If you plan to connect something to a TV or projector, please bring any adapters you need.

## Code Of Conduct

We want Fight Night to stay friendly, welcoming, and fun. Please:

- Keep competition respectful
- Ask before touching someone else's controller, laptop, handheld, or setup
- Ask before recording, photographing, or streaming someone else's gameplay
- Respect different skill levels and play styles
- Help newer players feel welcome
- Keep advice friendly if another player asks for it

Speak up if anything makes you uncomfortable. We want to address issues early and keep the space good for everyone.
