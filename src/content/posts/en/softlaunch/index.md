**Secret Shop is starting its soft launch.** 

During the soft launch, Secret Shop will begin opening for regular Open Shop hours, casual drop-ins, project work, games, and weekend community events.

Secret Shop is still a space in progress, so this period is partly about testing what works: open hours, events, pricing, layout, community flow, and how people want to use the space.

If you make things, want to meet other creators, or are just curious about the space, you are welcome to stop by.

## What Can I Do During Open Shop?

Open Shop is a relaxed drop-in time.

You can use the space to:

- Work on a game or software project
- Draw, animate, write, compose, or design
- Build or test hardware projects
- Ask questions
- Meet other creators
- Share something you are working on
- Play games
- Study or practice a tool
- Find collaborators
- Hang out in a creative environment

You do not need to be a professional developer or artist.

You do not need to bring a finished project.

Curiosity is enough.

## Private Bookings

Outside of Open Shop hours and scheduled events, Secret Shop may also be available for private use.

Private bookings are intended for creative, technical, educational, and maker-focused groups whose activities fit the spirit of the Secret Shop community.

Examples include:

- Team meetings
- Study groups
- Creative workshops
- Game development teams
- Art or animation sessions
- Hardware projects
- Board game design groups
- Language exchange for creators
- Film, 3D, or animation groups

Booking information and pricing will be announced later.
